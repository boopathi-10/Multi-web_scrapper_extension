Privacy Policy for ScrapeFlow

Last Updated: November 2026

ScrapeFlow ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how ScrapeFlow handles user data.

1. Data Collection & Transmission

ScrapeFlow DOES NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data or browsing activity.

  • All data extraction and web scraping occurs exclusively client-side inside your browser.
  • No scraped web content, extracted tables, URLs, or search queries are sent to external servers or third-party analytics services.
  • The extension does not track your online identity, cookies, accounts, or browsing history.

2. Browser Permissions & Justification

ScrapeFlow requires only minimal permissions strictly necessary to execute user-initiated data scraping:

  • activeTab: Allows the extension to interact with the currently focused tab only when you explicitly click the extension action icon. It does not grant perpetual access across all websites.
  • scripting: Enables the injection of the visual element inspector and table parser into the active webpage upon request.
  • storage: Used solely to store your local preferences (such as CSV delimiter choices) and temporary preview datasets in your browser's chrome.storage.local.
  • downloads: Allows you to save your extracted datasets locally as CSV or JSON files on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

ScrapeFlow contains no third-party advertisements, tracking pixels, telemetry libraries, or external analytical scripts.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store developer contact page.
